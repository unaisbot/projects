Who We Are

About TBARC India

TBARC India is an organisation focused on biodiversity, ecology, environmental research and conservation. Through scientific projects, field-based investigations and collaborative initiatives, TBARC aims to contribute to the understanding and conservation of natural ecosystems.

Our activities encompass biodiversity documentation, ecological research, invasive species studies, environmental monitoring and scientific knowledge sharing. We encourage participation and collaboration among researchers, students, institutions and nature enthusiasts.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To contribute towards a scientifically informed society that values biodiversity, understands ecological processes and supports environmental conservation.

Our Mission

To promote biodiversity research, ecological documentation, conservation initiatives and scientific collaboration through meaningful research and knowledge dissemination.
